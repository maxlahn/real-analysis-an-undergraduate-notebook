Definition 4.1.7. Subspace topology.

The subspace topology \(\rest{ \tau }_{ U } \) induced on a subset \(U \subseteq X \) of a topological space \(\of{ X , \tau } \) is the collection
\begin{equation*} \rest{ \tau }_{ U } = \setmid{ U \cap V \in \ps{ X } }{ V \in \tau }\text{.} \end{equation*}
This collection \(\rest{ \tau }_{ U } \) is a topology on \(U \) by Lemma 4.1.6 Subspace topology, and the topological space \(\of{ U , \rest{ \tau }_{ U } } \) is called a (topological) subspace of \(\of{ X , \tau } \text{.}\)
