Definition 3.4.7. Interior; closure; boundary.

Fix a subset \(U \subseteq X \) of a metric space \(\of{ X , \rho } \text{.}\)
  • Interior.
    A point \(x \in X \) is an interior point of \(U \) if \(U \) is a neighborhood of \(x \text{.}\) The set of interior points of \(U \) is called the interior of \(U \) and is denoted \(\Int \of{ U } \) or \(U^{ \circ } \text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • Closure.
    A point \(x \in X \) is called a point of closure of \(U \) in \(X \) if \(N \cap U \neq \emptyset \) for all neighborhoods \(N \) of \(x \) in \(X \text{.}\) The set of points of closure of \(U \) in \(X \) is called the closure of \(U \) in \(X \) and is denoted \(\Cl \of{ U } \) or \(\overline{ U } \text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • Boundary.
    A point \(x \in X \) is called a boundary point of \(U \) in \(X \) if \(N \cap U \neq \emptyset \) and \(N \setminus U \neq \emptyset \) for all neighborhoods \(N \) of \(x \) in \(X \text{.}\) The set of all boundary points of \(U \) in \(X \) is called the boundary of \(U \) in \(X \) and is denoted \(\Bd \of{ U } \) or \(\partial U \text{.}\)
