Definition 4.1.4. Metric topology.

The metric topology \(\tau_{ \rho } \) induced on a set \(X \) by a metric \(\rho \colon X \times X \to \R \) on \(X \) is the collection of open subsets in \(X \text{.}\) That \(\tau_{ \rho } \) is a topology on \(X \) follows immediately from (1), (2), and (4) of Proposition 3.4.10 Topology of a metric space.
🔗
A topology \(\tau \) on a set \(X \) is called metrizable if it is the metric topology induced by a metric on \(X \text{,}\) and non-metrizable otherwise. A topological space \(\of{ X , \tau } \) is called metrizable (resp. non-metrizable) if the topology is metrizable (resp. non-metrizable).
