Definition 5.4.4. Strict asymptotic dominance.

Let \(f , g \colon X \to \R \) be real-valued functions defined on a subset \(X \subseteq \R \text{,}\) and fix a limit point \(c \in X' \text{.}\) We say that \(f \) is strictly asymptotically dominated by \(g \) near \(c \) if \(f \preccurlyeq g \) but \(f \not \asymp g \) as \(x \to c \text{.}\) In this case, we write \(f \prec g \) as \(x \to c \text{.}\)
