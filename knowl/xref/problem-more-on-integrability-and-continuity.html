Problem 1. More on integrability and continuity.

Let \(f \colon \clint{ a }{ b } \to \R \) be a Riemann integrable real-valued function defined on a closed interval. Prove that \(g \circ f \) is Riemann integrable for any continuous real-valued function \(g \colon \R \to \R \text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Use the Heine-Cantor theorem on an appropriate restriction of \(g \text{.}\)
