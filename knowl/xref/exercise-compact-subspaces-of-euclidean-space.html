Exercise Group 3.6.4.7–10. Compact subspaces of Euclidean space.

Determine whether or not the given subset of Euclidean space is compact.
🔗

7.

Let \(U \) be the subset of the Euclidean plane \(\R^{ 2 } \) defined by
\begin{equation*} U = \setmid{ \vect{ x } \in \R^{ 2 } }{ \vect{ x } \neq \vect{ 0 } }\text{.} \end{equation*}
Determine whether or not \(U \) is compact in \(\R^{ 2 } \text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

8.

Let \(V \) be the subset of \(3 \)-dimensional Euclidean space \(\R^{ 3 } \) defined by
\begin{equation*} V = \setmid{ \vect{ x } \in \R^{ 3 } }{ \norm{ \vect{ x } } = 2 }\text{.} \end{equation*}
Determine whether or not \(V \) is compact.
🔗
🔗

9.

Determine whether or not the irrational numbers \(\R \setminus \Q \) are compact.

🔗

10.

Let \(a , b \in \R \) be real numbers, and suppose that \(a \leq b \text{.}\) Determine whether or not the closed interval \(\clint{ a }{ b } \) is compact.
