Problem 1. Open and closed sets in metric subspaces.

Let \(U \subseteq X \) be a subset of a metric space \(\of{ X , \rho } \text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

Prove that a subset \(V \subseteq U \) is open in the metric subspace \(\of{ U , \rho } \) if and only if \(V = U \cap W \) for some open set \(W \subseteq X \) in \(X \text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

(b)

Prove that if a subset \(V \subseteq U \) is open in \(X \text{,}\) then it is open in \(U \text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

(c)

Construct an explicit counterexample to the converse of (b) above.
🔗
