Problem 3. Closure and limit points.

Let \(U \subseteq X \) be a subset of a metric space \(\of{ X , \rho } \text{.}\) Prove that \(\Cl \of{ U } = U \cup U' \text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Recall that the derived set \(U' \) of a subset \(U \subseteq X \) of a metric space \(\of{ X , \rho } \) is the set of all limit points of \(U \) in \(X \text{.}\)
