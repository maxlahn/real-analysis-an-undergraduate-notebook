Example 7.2.8. Applying the Alternating series test.

Consider a positive real number \(0 \lt p \lt \infty \) and the series
\begin{equation*} \sum_{ n = 1 }^{ \infty }{ \frac{ \of{ -1 }^{ n } }{ n^{ p } } }\text{.} \end{equation*}
Since \(\frac{ 1 }{ n^{ p } } \) is decreasing and converges to \(0 \text{,}\) the Alternating series test implies that the above series converges.
