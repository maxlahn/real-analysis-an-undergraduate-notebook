Theorem 6.3.16. Limit comparison test for integral convergence.

Let \(f , g \colon X \to \R \) be real-valued functions defined on an interval \(X \text{,}\) and suppose that \(f \asymp g \) as \(x \to \inf \of{ X } \) and \(x \to \sup \of{ X } \text{.}\) If the restriction of \(f \) to any compact subinterval of the interior \(\Int \of{ X } \) is integrable and \(g \) is improperly integrable, then \(f \) is improperly integrable.
