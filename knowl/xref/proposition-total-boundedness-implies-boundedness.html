Proposition 3.4.24. Total boundedness implies boundedness.

If a subset of a metric space is totally bounded, then it is bounded.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let \(U \subseteq X \) be a totally bounded subset of a metric space \(\of{ X , \rho } \text{.}\) Since \(1 \gt 0 \) is a positive real number,
\begin{equation*} U \subseteq \bigcup_{ k = 1 }^{ n }{ \mB_{ 1 } \of{ x_{ k } } } \end{equation*}
for some finite list of points \(x_{ 1 } , \dotsc , x_{ n } \in X \text{.}\) Lemma 3.4.14 Diameter and radius and Corollary 3.4.21 Unions of bounded sets together imply that \(U \) is bounded.
