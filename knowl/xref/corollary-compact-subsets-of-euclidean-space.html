Corollary 3.6.22. Compact subsets of Euclidean space.

A subset of Euclidean space is compact if and only if it is closed and bounded.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The Heine-Borel Theorem implies that a subset of Euclidean space is compact if and only if it is complete and totally bounded. Since Euclidean space is complete by Corollary 3.6.16 Completeness of the \(p \)-norm, Proposition 3.6.7 Closed subsets of complete metric spaces implies that a subset of Euclidean space is complete if and only if it is closed. Moreover, Proposition 3.4.25 Total boundedness in Euclidean space implies that a subset of Euclidean space is totally bounded if and only if it is bounded.
