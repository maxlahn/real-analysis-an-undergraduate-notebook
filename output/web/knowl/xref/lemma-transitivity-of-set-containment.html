Lemma 1.1.9. Transitivity of set containment.

For any sets \(U \text{,}\) \(V \text{,}\) and \(X \text{,}\) if \(U \subseteq V \) and \(V \subseteq X \text{,}\) then \(U \subseteq X \text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let \(u \in U \text{.}\) Since \(u \in U \) and \(U \subseteq V \text{,}\) \(u \in V \text{.}\) Moreover, since \(u \in V \) and \(V \subseteq X \text{,}\) \(u \in X \text{.}\) In summary, we have shown that for \(u \in X \) for all elements \(u \in U \text{,}\) and so \(U \subseteq X \text{.}\)
